# Catalog provenance

`src/plants/catalog.json` is generated by `scripts/plants/build-catalog.py` from raw extracts kept next to it. The zone guide and store were first read on 2026-11-02 and refreshed against Gardyn's plant library and yCube collection on 2026-11-16. Sprouting windows were read from the Plant Book and Iowa State Extension on 2026-11-17:

| Field | Source | Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| `zone` | Gardyn's placement-by-zone guide, plus Light Zone on plant-book articles for plants added later | all 141 (138 original guide lines; "Dragon Beans Green Beans" split into two plants; Pink Swiss Chard and Oopsy Daisy from the 2026-11-16 library) |
| `harvestMonths` | "First Harvest" on each yCube product page, or days-to-maturity on the plant-book article (converted at 30 days/month) | store or plant-book when published; otherwise the category median and `harvestEstimated` |
| `careLevel`, `yield` | "Care Level", "Yield" on product pages; Care Level on plant-book articles when the store has no value | store items plus plant-book care where published; yield is store-only (null otherwise) |
| `inStore`, `gardynHandle` | yCube collection | 75 sold on 2026-11-16 (Purslane and bare-root Strawberries were added to the shop after the Nov 2 extract) |
| `succession` | Gardyn's succession table groups | exact when named there, else by category with `successionInferred` |
| `sproutDays` | "Days to sprout" on the plant's Plant Book page (`gardyn-plant-book-sprout.txt`, read 2026-11-17), else "Days to Sprout" on its [help centre page](plant-pages.md) (`gardyn-help-plants.txt`), else its crop in Iowa State Extension's germination table (`isu-germination.txt`) | 137 (52 Plant Book, 85 help centre, 0 Iowa State), kept beside the plants by id; `categorySproutMedians` stands in for bare-root-strawberries and sorrel (`sproutWindow` flags it estimated) |
| `facts.thinTo` | the [thinning article](thinning.md)'s per-plant list, else the plant's page | 138; sorrel, Oopsy Daisy and Pink Swiss Chard fall back to `thinRule` for their kind |
| `category`, `size`, `placement` | rules in the build script | `size: large` = fruiting/vining or sunflower; `placement` = cucumber exception |

No prose is copied. The care and harvest notes in `src/plants/care.json` are rewritten in seedypod's own words ([plant pages](plant-pages.md)). Microgreens are excluded (Gardyn grows them in trays, not pods).

## Sprouting windows

The Plant Book comes first. On 2026-11-17 every plant without a Plant Book window was re-checked at `mygardyn.com/plant-book/<id>/`: each link 404s, lands on a yCube product page (no days to sprout), or lands on a family page with no days (`grow-flowers`, `grow-fruits`, `plants`). Bull's Blood Beets still lands on the kale page, so its Plant Book figure isn't used.

Since 2026-11-20 the help centre's plant pages fill the rest: they give Gardyn's own figure for each variety, which replaced Iowa State's one-figure-per-crop windows for the 52 plants that had them (kale went from 4–7 to 7–21, peppers from 7–10 to 7–14 or wider). Iowa State's table stays in the build as the last fallback but currently supplies no plant. Bare-root strawberries arrive already rooted and have no sprouting window. Sorrel's page gives none.

## Known gaps

- Harvest windows are months, not days; the sowing planner converts at 30 days per month.
- Succession gaps use the midpoint of Gardyn's ranges (10, 21, 31, 56 days); flowers have no fixed cadence.
- Gardyn's products change; re-run the extraction and the build script, then review the diff.
- Lime Basil and Mammoth Basil are in the plant library and zone guide but are not currently sold as yCubes; they stay `inStore: false` with published care and harvest from their plant-book articles.
