# Catalog provenance

`src/plants/catalog.json` is generated by `scripts/plants/build-catalog.py` from raw extracts kept next to it. The zone guide and store were first read on 2026-11-02 and refreshed against Gardyn's plant library and yCube collection on 2026-11-16:

| Field | Source | Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| `zone` | Gardyn's placement-by-zone guide, plus Light Zone on plant-book articles for plants added later | all 141 (138 original guide lines; "Dragon Beans Green Beans" split into two plants; Pink Swiss Chard and Oopsy Daisy from the 2026-11-16 library) |
| `harvestMonths` | "First Harvest" on each yCube product page, or days-to-maturity on the plant-book article (converted at 30 days/month) | store or plant-book when published; otherwise the category median and `harvestEstimated` |
| `careLevel`, `yield` | "Care Level", "Yield" on product pages; Care Level on plant-book articles when the store has no value | store items plus plant-book care where published; yield is store-only (null otherwise) |
| `inStore`, `gardynHandle` | yCube collection | 75 sold on 2026-11-16 (Purslane and bare-root Strawberries were added to the shop after the Nov 2 extract) |
| `succession` | Gardyn's succession table groups | exact when named there, else by category with `successionInferred` |
| `category`, `size`, `placement` | rules in the build script | `size: large` = fruiting/vining or sunflower; `placement` = cucumber exception |

No prose is copied: care notes are not in the catalog. Microgreens are excluded (Gardyn grows them in trays, not pods).

## Known gaps

- Harvest windows are months, not days; the sowing planner converts at 30 days per month.
- Succession gaps use the midpoint of Gardyn's ranges (10, 21, 31, 56 days); flowers have no fixed cadence.
- Gardyn's products change; re-run the extraction and the build script, then review the diff.
- Lime Basil and Mammoth Basil are in the plant library and zone guide but are not currently sold as yCubes; they stay `inStore: false` with published care and harvest from their plant-book articles.
